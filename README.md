Privacy Policy — XQC (XiangQi Career)
Last updated: 1 August 2026
XQC (“the app”, “XiangQi Career”) is developed by WAR25 Studio (“we”, “us”).
This policy explains what the app does with your information. It is short
because the app collects nothing.
We do not collect any personal data
The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal or
sensitive user data. Specifically, it does not collect:
names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account identifiers
device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP addresses
location data
contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera input
analytics, crash telemetry, or usage statistics
The app requests no Android permissions at all.
Your game data stays on your device
Your player profile, career progress, ratings, match history, settings, and
unlock status are saved only in the app’s private storage on your device.
We cannot see this data and it is never uploaded anywhere.
If you uninstall the app, Android deletes this data. You can also clear it at
any time via Android Settings → Apps → XQC → Storage → Clear data.
No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics
The app contains no advertising, no advertising SDKs, no tracking pixels, and
no third-party analytics services.
No internet connection
The app is fully offline. It does not connect to the internet, and it holds no
network permission, so it cannot send data off your device.
Purchases
If the app offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock the full
game, that purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never
see or receive your payment details. Google’s handling of that transaction is
covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.
The app only stores whether the full version is unlocked.
Children
The app is a Chinese chess (xiangqi) game and is suitable for general
audiences. Because we collect no data from anyone, we collect no data from
children.
Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and post
the revised version at this same address.
